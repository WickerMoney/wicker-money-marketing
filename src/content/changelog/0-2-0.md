Tell Wicker Money what you expect to come in and go out, and it tells you
whether you'll make it to payday. **Back up your database before upgrading:**
this release reshapes how recurring items are stored, and going back to 0.1.0
means restoring that backup.

### Added

- **Recurring items.** A Recurring page for your paychecks, bills, card and
  loan payments, and transfers between your own accounts. Each one shows when
  it's next due, in plain words ("Every 2 weeks"), and the page totals what
  comes in, what goes out and what's left each month. A paycheck split across
  two accounts is one item, not two.
- **Until payday.** A dashboard widget that answers "will I make it?": how
  much is safe to spend before your next paycheck, each checking account's
  lowest point before then, and a warning naming any account that would drop
  below the cushion you set for it. One account's extra money is never
  quietly counted against another account's shortfall, because your bank
  doesn't do that either.
- **Choose what counts as spendable.** Pick which accounts count toward
  "safe to spend", and set each account's cushion (its buffer), on the
  Accounts page.
- Deleting or merging an account now tells you which recurring items it
  affects, and what will happen to each, before anything changes.

### Fixed

- Plugins showed dates a day early for anyone west of UTC.

### Known limitation

- "Today" followed UTC, so in the evening in the Americas the Recurring page
  and the widget had already moved on to tomorrow. Fixed in 0.2.1.
