Pick the plugins your instance runs, match payments straight from your
transactions, and see form mistakes right where you made them. **Back up your
database before upgrading.** Both of this release's database changes can be
undone, so going back to 0.3.0 doesn't need the backup, but it's still the
safe habit.

**If more than one person signed up on your instance before this release,
read this:** from now on only the first account is the instance's owner, and
everyone who signs up later is a member. Existing accounts keep the role they
had, and before this release that was always owner. The
[self-hosting docs](https://wickermoney.dev/docs/features/owners-and-members)
show how to check and change it.

### Added

- **Turn plugins on and off.** Settings → Plugins lists every plugin, what it
  adds, and whether it's on. The instance's owner can switch any of them on or
  off, and the change shows up straight away, no reload. Turning a plugin off
  deletes nothing: turn it back on and everything is where you left it.
  Plugins that do similar jobs, such as two ways of budgeting, can run side by
  side.
- **Owners and members.** The first account is the owner, the only one who
  can change which plugins are on. Everyone else uses the app as normal, with
  their own data.
- **Match from the Transactions page.** A Recurring column shows which
  recurring item each transaction paid, suggests one to confirm with a click,
  or lets you pick another.
- **"Not this."** Dismiss a suggested match you know is wrong and it won't
  come back. Changed your mind? Undo.
- **Errors where you'll see them.** Every form now checks what it can before
  sending, and shows each problem under the field it's about, in plain
  sentences, instead of in a message at the top of the page.

### Changed

- The Transactions pager shows "Page 3 of 25" once it knows the total.
- Building from source needs a newer Node.js (22.22.2 or 24.15 and up). The
  container image is unaffected.

### Fixed

- "Only uncategorized" no longer lists transfers between your own accounts,
  which can't take a category, so an empty list now means everything is
  filed.
- Dark mode no longer flashes white on every page load.
