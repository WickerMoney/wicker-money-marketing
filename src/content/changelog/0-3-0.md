Tell Wicker Money when a paycheck or bill actually posts, and it stops
counting it as still to come. Plus budgets that cover a stretch of time
instead of a single month. **Back up your database before upgrading:** going
back to 0.2.1 means restoring that backup. Needs PostgreSQL 15 or later; 16 was
already the minimum.

### Added

- **Did these land?** Match a transaction to the recurring item it paid, and
  Until payday and the forecast stop counting it twice. A paycheck that lands
  a day early is no longer counted again on payday. The Recurring page suggests
  likely matches; nothing is matched until you click.
- **One-off changes.** Skip one month's bill, move one paycheck to another
  day, or change one occurrence's amount, without touching the rest of the
  series. Each item's History shows its recent and coming dates and what paid
  each one.
- **Late payments.** Once you've matched an item, a payment that hasn't shown
  up within a week of its date is flagged late and still counted, so a delayed
  paycheck doesn't make your outlook look better than it is. Items you never
  match work exactly as before.
- **Budget windows.** One amount for one category across a date range, spent
  down to zero, like holiday gifts from October 1 to December 25. You see
  "spent so far of funded" for the whole window, and bunched-up spending is
  expected, so it never gets flagged as at risk.
- For plugin authors: one shared, exact money module in the SDK.

### Changed

- A budget amount with more than four decimal places is now refused instead
  of quietly cut short.
