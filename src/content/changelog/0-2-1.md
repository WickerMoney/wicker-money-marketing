See where an account is heading, not just where it is. No database changes,
so upgrading from 0.2.0 is a pull and a restart. Afterwards, open
**Settings → Time zone**.

### Added

- **Forecast.** A new page that projects one account's balance day by day from
  your recurring items: 30, 60 or 90 days, 6 months, or to the end of the
  year. It shows zero and your cushion as lines, names the first day the
  balance would dip below either, and lists what moves the line. Bills on
  payday are drawn before the paycheck, so a tight day shows as the dip it
  is. It covers your recurring items, not everyday spending, and says so.
- **Your time zone.** Set it under Settings, so "today" turns over at your
  midnight, not UTC's. New accounts pick up your browser's time zone when you
  sign up.

### Fixed

- The Recurring page and Until payday no longer roll over to tomorrow in the
  evening once your time zone is set.
