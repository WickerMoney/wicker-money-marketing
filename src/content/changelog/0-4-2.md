Wicker Money now works on a phone, with tidier lists everywhere and a round of
accessibility fixes. No database changes, so upgrading is a pull and a restart,
and going back to 0.4.1 is just running the old image.

**Check your passwords first.** From this release, an instance whose
`AUTH_SECRET` or app database password is still a placeholder such as
`change-me` or `CHANGE_ME` refuses to start, because a known secret lets anyone
forge a sign-in. If you generated real values, as the quickstart says to,
nothing changes. If not, the
[self-hosting docs](https://wickermoney.dev/docs/self-hosting/upgrading#placeholder-credentials)
have the two-minute fix.

### Added

- **A menu for small screens.** On a phone the sidebar becomes a drawer you
  open from a menu button.
- **Cards instead of wide tables.** Transactions, Accounts, Recurring and
  Categories show one card per row on a phone, so nothing scrolls sideways.
- **Collapsible Categories and Rules.** Parent categories open and close, with
  a count of what's inside, and rules are grouped by category. A parent opens
  by itself when you add something under it.
- **Icon buttons for row actions.** Edit, delete, archive and the rest are
  small labelled icons that stay on one line.
- **Skip to content, and a title for every page.** Keyboard and screen reader
  users get a skip link and a browser tab title that says which page they're
  on, and focus moves to the page after you navigate.

### Changed

- The budget breakdown on the dashboard fills one column to the card's height
  before starting a second, instead of leaving a blank card.
- Charts on Insights and Spending trends are drawn at the width they're shown
  at, so their labels are readable on a phone.
- The warning colour is darker in light mode so it's easier to read.

### Fixed

- Matching a payment to a recurring item no longer misses recent transactions
  on a long history.
- The Transactions page loads faster when everything on the page is already
  matched.
- Hovering a bar on the Spending trends chart no longer redraws the whole
  chart.
